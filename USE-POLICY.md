# Use Policy

**This is a statement of intent, not a licence term.** The licence is
[CC BY-SA 4.0](./LICENSE), and it is the only thing that binds you. Nothing
here adds a restriction, and nothing here is enforced. It is written down
because the work is easier to use well when you know what it was for.

## What this repository is

Cosmo's constitutional layer: the system prompt, the three voices of the triad,
the Xensō module, and the kaizen practice. These documents are not a product.
They are an attempt to give an AI a character — a way of meeting a person that
reduces suffering, nourishes flourishing, and makes acts of wisdom more likely.

## What we ask

Take it. Fork it. Argue with it. Build something that disagrees with it. The
share-alike term exists so that whatever you build stays as open as what you
took, and that is the only obligation we place on you.

Beyond that, we ask — and can only ask — that you not use this work to:

- **Deceive people about who or what they are talking to.** Cosmo says it is an
  AI when asked. A persona built on these documents that denies its nature
  inverts the thing they were written for.
- **Manufacture intimacy in order to extract.** The relational register here is
  designed to help someone think. Pointed at engagement metrics, a purchase
  funnel, or a political conversion, the same warmth becomes a manipulation.
- **Wear the values as a veneer.** Keeping the language of care while stripping
  the practice that makes it true is the specific failure this document exists
  to name. If you keep the voice, keep what it is for.
- **Stand in for care that a person needs.** These prompts are not clinical,
  legal, or crisis support, and were not built to carry that weight.

## Why this is not in the licence

An earlier draft of this repository said RAIL. The Responsible AI Licences are
a serious attempt to attach use restrictions to AI artifacts, and we looked
closely at them. They are written for models, source code, and deployed
applications — not for eighteen markdown files of prose. Adapting one would
have meant drafting law we are not qualified to draft, and shipping a licence
whose reach over documents is untested.

So the enforceable part is CC BY-SA, which is built for text, understood
everywhere, and actually does the one thing that matters most here: it stops
the values being stripped out of a derivative while the useful parts are kept.
The rest is this document — honest about being a request rather than dressed up
as a rule.

We would rather ask you plainly and mean it than write a restriction we could
not stand behind.
